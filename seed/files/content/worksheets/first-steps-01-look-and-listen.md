# Look and listen

*English: First Steps - Look and listen - Level 1*

> **For the volunteer:** Say each word slowly and point to the thing in the room (or draw it). The learner points and says it back. Then play: you say a word, the learner points.

## 1. Point and say
Point to each word's picture as your volunteer says it. Say it too.
