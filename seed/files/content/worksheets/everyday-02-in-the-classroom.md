# In the classroom

*English: Everyday - In the classroom - Level 6*

> **For the volunteer:** Play 'teacher says': give an instruction (sit down, stand up, open your book, put your hand up) and the learner does it. Then swap. Practise asking for help: 'Can you say it again, please?'

## 1. Label the things
Draw each thing and write its name: pencil, ruler, book, bag, scissors, chair.

## 2. Match the instruction
Draw a line from each instruction to what you do.

| instruction | what you do |
|---|---|
| Open your book. | Use your ears. |
| Listen. | Use your pencil. |
| Write your name. | Put your hand in the air. |
| Put your hand up. | Show the pages. |

## 3. Asking for help
Copy and say: I don't understand. Can you say it again, please?
